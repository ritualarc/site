Ritual Arc Intelligence Platform

Exploring the behavioural life of fashion through cultural observation, emotional relevance, and future-facing insight.

The Missing Middle

Between purchase and sustained wear

Ritual Arc examines the behavioural patterns that unfold between the moment a garment is bought and the years it may (or may not) remain in active use — looking closely at how clothing becomes memorable, neglected, rediscovered, or abandoned.

Conceptual Pathways

Three frameworks guide the research

Rewear

Exploring continued engagement and the emotional bonds people form with clothing over time.

Refine

Examining feedback, usability, and the evolving relationship between wearer and garment.

Redirect

Investigating repair, redistribution, and alternative futures for garments in decline.

Intended Impact

What this work is for

  • Connection. Strengthened emotional connections between people and their clothing.
  • Longevity. Extended periods of active garment use and visibility.
  • Responsiveness. More responsive brand-wearer relationships.
  • Design. Behaviourally-informed fashion system design.

Featured Insight

Passive Wardrobe Drift

“The gradual loss of visibility, relevance, and emotional connection between people and the clothing they already own.”
