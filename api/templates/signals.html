Cultural & Behavioural Signals

Quiet Integrity

Consumers appear more responsive to lived experience and behavioural authenticity than highly visible moral positioning. Where one approach seeks visibility for ethical claims, the other demonstrates trustworthiness through consistent action.

  • Hospitality. Functions as an expression of integrity through behaviour.
  • Trust. Develops through repeated consumer experiences rather than assertions.
  • Lived experience. Serves as a behavioural signal in its own right.
